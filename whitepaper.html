2. Proof-of-Inference (PoI) Consensus Engine

Traditional Proof-of-Work (PoW) burns electrical power on non-functional cryptographic hashes. In contrast, Proof-of-Inference (PoI) harnesses electrical energy for deterministic artificial intelligence execution:

[STEP 1: QUERY SHARDING]

A client application submits an inference payload. The gateway partitions the model weights (e.g., DeepSeek-R1 or Qwen-2.5) across available peer nodes.

[STEP 2: ZERO-KNOWLEDGE SAMPLING]

A randomized verification matrix is injected into the attention heads to confirm true hardware execution.

[STEP 3: FRACTIONAL $DEAI BLOCK REWARD]

Upon activation verification, the smart contract emits protocol emissions directly to the worker node's on-chain keypair.
